# Causal Validation: Assumptions and Limitations

## Assumptions
1. No important unmeasured confounders.
2. Customers have a meaningful chance of receiving each
   relevant discount option.
3. Treatment and outcome are correctly recorded.
4. Customer observations are sufficiently independent.

## Validation
- Missing values checked.
- Treatment group distribution checked.
- Unusual numeric values checked.
- DoWhy refutation tests attempted.
- Stability across repeated samples examined.

## Limitations
- Unmeasured customer preferences may affect results.
- Small treatment groups may produce unstable estimates.
- Historical data may not represent future campaigns.
- Estimated effects are not guaranteed business outcomes.
