# Personalized Discount Optimization

## Business Objective
Maximize the total expected incremental benefit
from personalized customer discounts.

## Discount Choices
- 0: No discount
- 10: Discount of 10 currency units
- 20: Discount of 20 currency units

## Budget
Total campaign discount budget: 1000 currency units.

## Expected Benefit
Expected incremental benefit =
Estimated treatment effect × Previous customer spend.

## Constraints
1. Each customer receives at most one offer.
2. Total discount cost must not exceed the budget.
3. Negative-benefit offers are not selected.
4. No-offer is always an available choice.
